# BOM notes

- Every line is priced in indicative USD for one reference site (three tilt stakes 10 m apart, one crack gauge, 60 m of bus cable, node on an existing pole; line 8 mast priced as a site option), with a supplier or supplier type. Prices are not quotes.
- Line numbers match the numbered callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Lines 9 and 10 have no callout.
- Line 6 (FieldNode core, $126) is the lab's shared node, costed in the FieldNode project under SLW-DDR-001 D1 (decided by Amish, 2026-09-25, SLW-DDR-002). It is listed for completeness and is not counted against the SlopeWatch budget.
- Reference site (SLW-DDR-002): the node and alert unit go on an existing pole, so line 8 (mast and footing, $28; the prototype's 48.3 mm test-slope mast) is a site option outside the reference-site cost. `budget_usd` ($250) is a hypothetical value-engineering target, not a limit. Value-engineering target: USD 250. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 304.00 for the SlopeWatch-specific parts of the reference site (USD 54.00 over the target); $332.00 at a site that needs the 48.3 mm mast. With the FieldNode core the reference site comes to $430.00. `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` sums the file and checks it against `project.yaml`.
- DDR-002 change: line 7 now includes a 26.9 mm post and a 7 m lead in conduit, so the keyed silence switch stands about 5 m from the siren (+$8, from $25 to $33).
- TRL 3 changes: line 4 adds a reader board and box (+$6), because the gauge is about 60 m of cable from the node; line 10 adds foam plugs for the capsule (+$1); line 2 specifies the SCL3300-D01 in mode 1 and a 0.4 m capsule depth; line 8 adds the earth rod to the description at no change in price.
- A LoRaWAN gateway is not included. Sites without coverage need one (TwinKit, about $290 in parts per its TRL 3 BOM).
- Design for construction (SLW-DDR-003, 2026-10-01): line 1 adds the screwed end cap and stand tube ($9 to $13); line 2 the centring collars ($27 to $27.50); line 3 becomes a head from stock drainage fittings with two conduit fittings and lever connectors ($5 to $11); line 4 adds clamp blocks, ball joints, a rod coupling, a folded guard and pegs ($38 to $46); line 7 adds the alert unit's back plate, V-blocks and band clamps and the switch back plate and hose clips ($33 to $43); line 8 adds a top cap and bonding clamps ($24 to $28); line 10 adds tape and a field-wireable plug ($9 to $11). The reference site rises from $245.00 to $296.50.
- Decided on 2026-10-02 (SLW-DEC-001) and now in `bom.csv`: line 3 stake heads in signal amber with a 25 mm retroreflective band, a stake ID label and a downslope arrow (+$2.50 a stake, $11.00 to $13.50); four 6.5 mm wall holes in the alert unit back plate (no change in price); line 5 and line 7 name the M12 pin assignment (port A pins 1 to 4, port B pins 1, 3 and 5). Lines 11 to 14 are site options priced on the same day and outside every total: 11 the 60.3 mm site mast ($37.00, replacing line 8 at a site installation, +$9.00), 12 a second alert unit at a machinery site ($64.00), 13 armoured bus cable per 10 m across a rockfall zone ($38.00), 14 an alert unit battery if FieldNode has not rated its 12 V rail ($20.00).
